# Financial Terms & KPI Glossary

## Profitability & Income Statement Metrics

### Revenue from Operations
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** Total Revenue, Revenue, Sales
- **Definition:** Total gross inflow of cash or receivables generated from selling core goods or rendering services during an accounting period, prior to deducting any operational expenses.
- **Formula:** `Units Sold * Selling Price per Unit`
- **Required Variables:** Units Sold, Selling Price per Unit

### Cost of Goods Sold
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** COGS, Cost of Sales
- **Definition:** Direct expenses incurred to produce or acquire products sold by the business, including raw material purchases, direct manufacturing labor, and freight-in costs.
- **Formula:** `Beginning Inventory + Direct Purchases - Ending Inventory`
- **Required Variables:** Beginning Inventory, Direct Purchases, Ending Inventory

### Gross Profit
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** Gross Earnings
- **Definition:** Residual earnings remaining after subtracting direct manufacturing or procurement costs from total operating revenue.
- **Formula:** `Revenue - COGS`
- **Required Variables:** Revenue, COGS

### Gross Margin
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** Gross Profit Margin
- **Definition:** Percentage of revenue retained as profit after covering direct production costs, reflecting core manufacturing or sourcing efficiency.
- **Formula:** `(Gross Profit / Revenue) * 100`
- **Required Variables:** Gross Profit, Revenue

### Operating Expenses
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** OpEx, SG&A
- **Definition:** Indirect operational expenditures required to maintain business activities, including administrative salaries, sales and marketing, research and development, and rent.
- **Formula:** `SG&A Expenses + R&D Expenses + Other Indirect Expenses`
- **Required Variables:** SG&A Expenses, R&D Expenses, Other Indirect Expenses

### Operating Profit
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** EBIT, Operating Income
- **Definition:** Earnings Before Interest and Taxes; measures profits derived purely from core operational activities, excluding financing structure and tax liabilities.
- **Formula:** `Gross Profit - OpEx`
- **Required Variables:** Gross Profit, OpEx

### EBITDA
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** Operating Cash Profit
- **Definition:** Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization; evaluates operational cash profitability by adding non-cash accounting adjustments back to operating earnings.
- **Formula:** `EBIT + Depreciation + Amortization`
- **Required Variables:** EBIT, Depreciation, Amortization

### Operating Margin
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** EBIT Margin, Operating Profit Margin
- **Definition:** Proportion of top-line revenue converted into operational profit, indicating overhead cost control and core business profitability.
- **Formula:** `(EBIT / Revenue) * 100`
- **Required Variables:** EBIT, Revenue

### Profit Before Tax
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** PBT, EBT
- **Definition:** Profitability level achieved after deducting operating expenses, non-cash depreciation charges, and net interest expenses, immediately preceding income tax liabilities.
- **Formula:** `EBIT - Interest Expense`
- **Required Variables:** EBIT, Interest Expense

### Net Income
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** Net Profit, PAT, Profit After Tax, Profit for the period
- **Definition:** Profit After Tax; the final bottom-line earnings available to shareholders after satisfying all operational costs, debt service obligations, and tax liabilities.
- **Formula:** `PBT - Tax Expense`
- **Required Variables:** PBT, Tax Expense

### Net Margin
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** Net Profit Margin, PAT Margin
- **Definition:** Percentage of revenue retained as net profit after deducting all operational, financial, and tax costs.
- **Formula:** `(Net Income / Revenue) * 100`
- **Required Variables:** Net Income, Revenue

### Earnings Per Share
- **Category:** Profitability
- **Aliases:** EPS, Basic EPS
- **Definition:** Net profit allocated to each individual outstanding share of common stock over a reporting period.
- **Formula:** `(Net Income - Preferred Dividends) / Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding`
- **Required Variables:** Net Income, Preferred Dividends, Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding


## Liquidity & Balance Sheet Metrics

### Current Assets
- **Category:** Liquidity
- **Aliases:** Short-Term Assets
- **Definition:** Short-term economic resources owned by a firm that are expected to be converted into cash or consumed within 12 months or one operating cycle.
- **Formula:** `Cash & Equivalents + Trade Receivables + Inventories + Other Short-Term Assets`
- **Required Variables:** Cash & Equivalents, Trade Receivables, Inventories, Other Short-Term Assets

### Current Liabilities
- **Category:** Liquidity
- **Aliases:** Short-Term Obligations
- **Definition:** Short-term financial obligations due for settlement within 12 months or one operating cycle.
- **Formula:** `Trade Payables + Short-Term Debt + Other Current Liabilities`
- **Required Variables:** Trade Payables, Short-Term Debt, Other Current Liabilities

### Working Capital
- **Category:** Liquidity
- **Aliases:** Net Working Capital, NWC
- **Definition:** Net short-term capital available to fund day-to-day business operations and near-term obligations.
- **Formula:** `Current Assets - Current Liabilities`
- **Required Variables:** Current Assets, Current Liabilities

### Current Ratio
- **Category:** Liquidity
- **Aliases:** Working Capital Ratio
- **Definition:** A fundamental liquidity metric testing whether short-term assets are sufficient to satisfy short-term liabilities.
- **Formula:** `Current Assets / Current Liabilities`
- **Required Variables:** Current Assets, Current Liabilities

### Quick Ratio
- **Category:** Liquidity
- **Aliases:** Acid-Test Ratio
- **Definition:** Strict liquidity assessment measuring short-term debt coverage using only highly liquid assets (excluding illiquid inventory).
- **Formula:** `(Cash & Equivalents + Marketable Securities + Trade Receivables) / Current Liabilities`
- **Required Variables:** Cash & Equivalents, Marketable Securities, Trade Receivables, Current Liabilities

### Trade Receivables
- **Category:** Liquidity
- **Aliases:** Accounts Receivable, AR
- **Definition:** Uncollected balance of money owed to the firm by customers for goods or services delivered on credit terms.
- **Formula:** `Sum of outstanding customer credit invoices`
- **Required Variables:** Accounts Receivable Balance

### Trade Payables
- **Category:** Liquidity
- **Aliases:** Accounts Payable, AP
- **Definition:** Outstanding balance of short-term debt owed by the company to vendors or suppliers for goods and services received on credit.
- **Formula:** `Sum of outstanding vendor credit bills`
- **Required Variables:** Accounts Payable Balance

### Property, Plant, and Equipment
- **Category:** Liquidity
- **Aliases:** PPE, Net PPE, Fixed Assets
- **Definition:** Long-term tangible assets critical to operations, reported net of accumulated depreciation.
- **Formula:** `Gross PPE - Accumulated Depreciation`
- **Required Variables:** Gross PPE, Accumulated Depreciation

### Shareholders' Equity
- **Category:** Liquidity
- **Aliases:** Total Equity, Net Worth, Book Value
- **Definition:** Residual claim on total business assets remaining after settling all outstanding liabilities.
- **Formula:** `Total Assets - Total Liabilities`
- **Required Variables:** Total Assets, Total Liabilities


## Solvency, Efficiency & Return Metrics

### Total Debt
- **Category:** Solvency
- **Aliases:** Gross Debt, Total Borrowings
- **Definition:** Aggregated financial obligations combining short-term obligations, current portions of long-term loans, and non-current borrowings.
- **Formula:** `Short-Term Debt + Long-Term Debt`
- **Required Variables:** Short-Term Debt, Long-Term Debt

### Debt-to-Equity Ratio
- **Category:** Solvency
- **Aliases:** D/E Ratio, Leverage Ratio
- **Definition:** Financial leverage indicator measuring the proportion of corporate financing funded by debt relative to equity capital.
- **Formula:** `Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity`
- **Required Variables:** Total Debt, Shareholders' Equity

### Interest Coverage Ratio
- **Category:** Solvency
- **Aliases:** Times Interest Earned, TIE
- **Definition:** Solvency indicator evaluating how comfortably operating earnings cover required interest payments on debt.
- **Formula:** `EBIT / Interest Expense`
- **Required Variables:** EBIT, Interest Expense

### Return on Equity
- **Category:** Efficiency
- **Aliases:** ROE
- **Definition:** Financial performance metric assessing how effectively management generates profits using shareholder capital.
- **Formula:** `(Net Income / Shareholders' Equity) * 100`
- **Required Variables:** Net Income, Shareholders' Equity

### Return on Capital Employed
- **Category:** Efficiency
- **Aliases:** ROCE
- **Definition:** Efficiency metric evaluating profitability relative to total capital actively invested in core operations.
- **Formula:** `(EBIT / (Total Assets - Current Liabilities)) * 100`
- **Required Variables:** EBIT, Total Assets, Current Liabilities

### Asset Turnover Ratio
- **Category:** Efficiency
- **Aliases:** Asset Turnover
- **Definition:** Efficiency metric measuring how effectively a company utilizes its total asset base to generate revenue.
- **Formula:** `Revenue / Average Total Assets`
- **Required Variables:** Revenue, Average Total Assets


## Cash Flow Metrics

### Operating Cash Flow
- **Category:** Cash Flow
- **Aliases:** CFO, Cash Flow from Operations
- **Definition:** Net cash generated or consumed directly by primary revenue-producing operational activities, adjusting net income for non-cash expenses and working capital changes.
- **Formula:** `Net Income + Non-Cash Items - Change in Working Capital`
- **Required Variables:** Net Income, Non-Cash Items, Change in Working Capital

### Capital Expenditures
- **Category:** Cash Flow
- **Aliases:** CapEx
- **Definition:** Cash spent acquiring, maintaining, or upgrading physical fixed assets such as property, plants, or equipment.
- **Formula:** `Ending Net PPE - Beginning Net PPE + Depreciation`
- **Required Variables:** Ending Net PPE, Beginning Net PPE, Depreciation

### Free Cash Flow
- **Category:** Cash Flow
- **Aliases:** FCF, Free Cash Flow to Firm
- **Definition:** Net operational cash remaining after funding necessary capital expenditures, available for debt repayment, dividends, or acquisitions.
- **Formula:** `Operating Cash Flow - CapEx`
- **Required Variables:** Operating Cash Flow, CapEx
